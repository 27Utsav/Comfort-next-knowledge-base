Services

Furnaces

Furnaces: installation, repair, maintenance. Gas (natural gas or propane), electric, and hybrid (furnace plus heat pump) systems. Same-day installation for most standard jobs (about 1 day). Typical furnace repairs take 1 to 2 hours depending on the issue.

Heat pumps

Heat pumps: sales, rentals, installation, repair, maintenance. Ducted, ductless (mini-split), and cold climate air source types. Efficiency described as "over 300%", meaning about three units of heat per unit of electricity used. Average lifespan 12 to 20 years with proper maintenance.

Air conditioning

Air conditioning: repair, installation, maintenance, rentals. Central, ductless mini-split, low-duct heat pump, and single-room units.

Boilers

Boilers: sales, rentals, installation, repair, maintenance. Conventional, condensing, and combi types. Standard installations take 1 to 2 days; complex conversions may take longer. Boiler systems described as lasting 20 to 30 years.

Air handlers

Air handlers: sales, rentals, installation, repair, maintenance, including diagnostics for motor, blower, control board, and airflow issues.

Fireplaces

Fireplaces: sales, rentals, installation, repair, maintenance. Gas fireplaces, gas fireplace inserts, gas stoves, and electric fireplaces (wall mounted, three sided, see through, built in, modern styles).

Water heaters

Water heaters: tank and tankless, sales and rentals, installation and 24/7 emergency repair, maintenance including flushing and anode rod replacement. Tank sizes available: 40, 50, and 60 gallon; gas (natural gas) and electric models.

Water products

Water products: water softeners, reverse osmosis systems, water quality consultation and testing.

Air quality

Air quality: humidifier installation, dehumidifier installation, HRV/ERV systems, UV light air purifiers, air cleaners, iWave air purification systems. Systems described as maintenance-free and ozone-free.

Solar panels

Solar panels: installation and system design, roof inspection and permits, grid connection, battery backup pairing, bifacial panel and full kit options, 1 to 2 day install timeframe.

Smart battery storage

Smart battery storage: whole home battery backup, 10kWh to 30kWh options (15 to 20kWh recommended for most homes), next-day installation, works with or without solar.

EV charger installation

EV charger installation: Level 2 chargers for residential and commercial, smart charging with usage tracking, scheduling and load balancing, servicing Tesla, Hyundai Ioniq, Ford Mustang Mach-E and other EVs.

Generators are mentioned as part of the energy product line on the homepage and about page.

All services offer free consultations and free quotes. Almost every service page repeats: "Satisfaction Guaranteed", "24/7 Service", "Fair Pricing" or "No hidden fees" and transparent, upfront pricing.
